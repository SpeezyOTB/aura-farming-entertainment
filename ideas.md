# Aura Farming Entertainment — Design Brainstorm

## Three Stylistic Approaches

### Option A — Mythic Ink
**Theme:** Dark parchment meets martial arts mythology. Brushstroke textures, ink-wash aesthetics, and ancient scroll energy.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Option B — Neon Dojo (Cyberpunk Anime)
**Theme:** Dark background, neon purple/green/red energy lines, glitch effects, anime-inspired typography.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Option C — Aura Pulse (Chosen)
**Theme:** Deep cosmic dark with vivid aura energy bursts — purple, green, and red power radiating from a dark void. Feels like raw energy contained in a sleek, modern entertainment brand.
**Probability:** 0.09

---

## Chosen Approach: Aura Pulse

**Design Movement:** Dark cosmic entertainment branding — think Marvel Studios meets anime power-up aesthetics. Bold, kinetic, and unmistakably powerful.

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep darkness as the canvas — near-black backgrounds let brand colors pop with maximum contrast
2. Aura energy as the visual metaphor — glowing halos, radial gradients, and light-bleed effects
3. Asymmetric layout tension — content anchored off-center to create dynamic visual flow
4. Every page feels like a portal into a world, not just a webpage

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep void black `oklch(0.08 0.01 285)`
- Primary Purple: Electric violet `oklch(0.55 0.28 290)` — power, mystery, the brand's soul
- Accent Green: Aura lime `oklch(0.72 0.22 140)` — life force, energy, vitality
- Accent Red: Dragon crimson `oklch(0.55 0.22 25)` — combat, passion, Dragon Fist X
- White: Pure luminance `oklch(0.97 0 0)` — text, clarity, contrast

**Layout Paradigm:** Diagonal slash sections, full-bleed hero panels, offset card grids. Navigation is a slim top bar that fades to transparent over hero content and solidifies on scroll.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Glowing aura rings — radial gradient halos behind key headings and CTAs
2. Diagonal color-slash dividers between sections
3. Animated particle/energy shimmer on hero backgrounds

**Interaction Philosophy:** Every hover reveals energy — buttons glow, cards lift with a subtle aura, links pulse with color. The site responds to the user like a living organism.

**Animation:** Entrance animations use upward fade-in with stagger (60ms apart). Hover states use 200ms ease-out glow transitions. Page transitions use a brief fade. Reduced-motion respected.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Rajdhani (bold, condensed, martial-arts energy) — headings
- Body: Inter (clean, readable) — paragraphs and UI
- Accent: Orbitron (futuristic) — labels, badges, nav items

**Brand Essence:** Aura Farming Entertainment — the creative studio powering next-generation anime games and TCG experiences. For fans who live in the energy. Bold. Mythic. Alive.

**Brand Voice:** Direct, hype-inducing, world-building. Headlines feel like battle cries. CTAs feel like invitations to power up.
- Example headline: "Your Aura. Your Legend."
- Example CTA: "Enter the Shadow Realm"
- Banned: "Welcome to our website", "Get started today"

**Wordmark & Logo:** A stylized "A" with an energy ring orbiting it — the letter formed from two diagonal slashes with a glowing aura halo. No text in the mark itself.

**Signature Brand Color:** Electric violet `oklch(0.55 0.28 290)` — unmistakably Aura Farming Entertainment.

## Style Decisions
- Dark theme throughout; no light mode toggle
- Glowing borders on interactive cards use the element's thematic color (purple for brand, green for Shadow Khan, red for Dragon Fist X)
- Section dividers use diagonal CSS clip-path, never horizontal lines
